Brand Identity

EYAS KIDS

Affiliated with EYAS International Education Group, EYAS American Kindergarten provides an authentic American-style immersive early childhood education tailored for children aged 2 to 6. Utilizing the DAP (Developmentally Appropriate Practice) international early childhood education system, it conducts inquiry-based teaching. The kindergarten actively fulfills the group's core mission of cultivating exploratory global talents, comprehensively enhancing children's overall qualities and bilingual abilities within a fully immersive all-English environment.
