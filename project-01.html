BOBO Taste Shop

BOBO Taste Shop is a space dedicated to sharing BoBo's personal tastes. The overall design is centered around the BoBo IP character, conveying the warmth of BoBo's home and his unique flavors to consumers. This approach gives the brand store a more human, approachable, and intimate feel.
