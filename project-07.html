Brand Identity

YOUNG CUBILOSE

A high-end traceable cubilose (bird's nest) brand covering full categories including dry bird's nest, blood bird's nest, and ready-to-eat products. Targeting urban young women, it focuses on youthful and light nourishment. With a convenient and pure product system, it simplifies the traditional nourishment process, allowing beauty and internal health adjustment to seamlessly blend into daily life.
